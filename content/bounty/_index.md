Reports submitted through private vendor Security Response Center (SRC) programs. These portals — Kuaishou SRC and JD SRC — have no public per-report URL, so each entry links to a screenshot of the platform's own vulnerability-detail page, showing the vendor's confirmation and acknowledgment of the report (status such as *Confirmed* or *Under repair*), viewable without an account. Broader manual bug-bounty history (Bugcrowd: 8 validated vulnerabilities, 100% accuracy, Hall of Fame) is linked from the [About](/about/) page.
